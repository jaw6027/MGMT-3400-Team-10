TECH 2 GO - COMMON REPAIR PARTS AND RETAIL PRICE EXAMPLES
Prepared: November 26, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)

Scope: 22 individually identified products covering common repair part types.
These are representative compatible examples, not universal parts, a sales-frequency
ranking, or a complete inventory for every phone/computer model. Older serviceable
models are included to make compatibility and sourcing concrete.

Prices are advertised US retail product prices in USD, accessed 2026-11-26.
Sources are official retailer pages retrieved through web search; some content
is cached. These are dated price observations, not checkout-verified quotations.
Labor, tax, shipping, travel, tools not included in the selected kit, and markup
are excluded. Kits and packs are priced as the stated retail unit, not per
component. Stock is the status shown on the product/evidence page, not reserved
inventory. Recheck exact variant, condition, price and stock before ordering.

The price is stored in supplier_listings.unit_price_cents; each product has its
own parts row. Computers retain the existing broad database category; Laptop /
Desktop prefixes on part_type distinguish them without changing the schema.
Unknown shipping/tax values remain NULL; no incomplete total is a lowest-price claim.
Supplier-wide verification remains unverified; this research checks named listings only.
